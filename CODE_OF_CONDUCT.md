# Code of Conduct

SoftBeat Atlas is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive
community. Participants must not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or
intimidate others. Keep discussions focused on the work, assume good faith,
and welcome different experience levels and perspectives.

Project maintainers may remove content, pause participation, or take other
appropriate action when behavior violates this standard. Report concerns to
the project maintainers through the private channel described in `SECURITY.md`.
